                      Tux Paint "Magic" Tool: Cross Stitch

                Written for this fork of Tux Paint 0.9.22 (2026)

   This turns the picture into embroidery: wherever you drag the mouse, it
   becomes fabric with an "x" of thread in each little square, the color of
   the picture there. White parts stay bare fabric, and even a thin line
   becomes a row of stitches.

   Its full-image mode embroiders the entire picture with one click.
